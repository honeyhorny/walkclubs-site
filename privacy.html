Walk Clubs

Privacy policy

Last updated 12 October 2026 (club boards, crash reports)

Walk Clubs is a walking game made by one person, Tummasuk Dechawanitcha, in Thailand ("we" below). This page says what the game keeps about you, why, who else handles it, and how to have it deleted. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps.

What stays on your phone

Most of the game lives on your phone: the fog you cleared today, your stamina, items, settings and photos you took. It is removed when you delete the app or use Delete account in Settings.

What we keep on our server

  • Your account. Every player gets an account id when the game first opens. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we also keep the email address they give us (Apple can give a private relay address instead) and which of the two you used. You can play as a guest without signing in.
  • Your profile. Your player name, club, how your Walker looks, your friend PIN and tag, your profile photo if you add one, today's step count and your streak. Only you and your friends can see it, except on the club boards: if you are among your club's top four walkers of the week, every player sees your name, your Walker's look and your steps that week (never your photo). You can leave the boards in Settings → Club boards.
  • Your steps each day, for the club boards.
  • Your friends. Who you added, and hearts given to friends' photos.
  • Photos you share. Photos you snap at Power Nodes, which only you and your friends can see. They are deleted 14 days after they were taken.
  • What you do at Power Nodes. Which node you charged or fortified, for which club, and when. These make the weekly scores and stop cheating.
  • Mist and core you earned and spent, and what you bought with them.
  • Your saved game, if you sign in. So it follows you to a new phone: your progress, badges, challenges, journal and the map cells (squares about 50 m wide) you have ever uncovered. Guests' games are not saved here.
  • Reports and blocks you make about other players.

Location

The game uses your location while it is open, to reveal the map around you and to check you are close enough to a Power Node to act on it. It never uses your location in the background. Your exact position is not stored on our server; it is sent only when you act on a node, to check the distance, and then dropped. The map cells in a signed-in player's saved game (above) are kept.

Steps and motion

The game reads your step count from your phone (Motion & Fitness on iPhone, Health Connect on Android) to spend stamina and climb the walk ladder. Only the count is used. Today's count is shown to your friends.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to scan a friend's QR code and to snap places you walk to; the photo library to pick a profile photo. Photos leave your phone only when you share them, and only your friends can see them. Please don't photograph people who haven't agreed to it.

Crash reports

When the game crashes or hits an error, it sends a report to Sentry: what went wrong, the game version and the kind of phone and system. It carries no account id, name, email or location, and is kept for up to 90 days.

Purchases

Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play, which handle your payment; we never see your card. RevenueCat keeps the record of what your account bought, under your account id, so purchases can be restored on a new phone.

Who else handles your data

  • Supabase runs our server and stores the data listed above.
  • RevenueCat keeps the record of purchases.
  • Sentry receives crash reports, stored in the EU.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in, payments and app delivery.
  • OpenFreeMap serves the map, and jsDelivr serves the code that draws it in 3D. Like any website, they see your device's IP address when the map loads.

Each of them handles data under its own privacy policy. Some of them may store it outside your country.

How long we keep it

Shared photos: 14 days. Everything else: until you delete your account.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete account removes your account, profile, photos, friends and saved game from our server, and the game from your phone. It can't be undone. Purchases stay with your App Store or Google Play account; to have RevenueCat's record of them deleted too, email us.
  • Permissions: you can turn off location, motion, camera or photos at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Ask us: to see, correct or delete your data, or with any question, email walkclubssupport@gmail.com. Under the GDPR, Thailand's PDPA and similar laws you can also object to how we use your data or complain to your data protection authority.

We use your data to run the game you asked for (our contract with you) and to keep it fair and safe (a legitimate interest).

Children

Walk Clubs is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly keep data about them. If you think a child has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be here with a new date, and the game will tell you about anything important.
