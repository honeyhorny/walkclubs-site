Walk Clubs

Walk · Claim · Win the week. A walking game where four clubs compete for the Power Nodes of your city.

Help

Questions, bugs or a report about a player: temmasuk@gmail.com. In the game, Settings → Send feedback adds your app version for us.

Report or block a player: open a friend on the Week page, tap ⋯, then Report or Block. Long-press a photo to report it.

Restore purchases: Store → Looks → Restore purchases, signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account.

Delete your account: Settings → Delete account.
